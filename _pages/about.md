I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Minnesota. My fields are **textual analysis and empirical macroeconomics**. I study when the text of news and research carries economic information that standard data miss, using large text corpora and machine-learning methods. **I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.**

In my [job market paper](/research/), I build text measures from 11.76 million articles in seven U.S. newspapers and ask whether, and when, they improve real-time forecasts of U.S. growth. My other work studies pricing and discount dynamics on the Steam video game platform, and the spread of identification designs across the economics profession.

Before Minnesota, I earned an M.Sc. in Economics from EIEF and LUISS (Rome) and a B.Sc. in Economics from Lomonosov Moscow State University. I teach undergraduate econometrics at Minnesota and have received the department's Distinguished Teaching Award in four semesters.
